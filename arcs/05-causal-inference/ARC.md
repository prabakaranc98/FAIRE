## What this arc is

The theoretical backbone: when can we identify cause from effect, intervene on a system,
or recover latent structure from observations alone?
This arc covers Pearl's do-calculus, structural causal models, causal representation learning,
and the identifiability conditions that separate what's learnable from what isn't.
The core question: *under what conditions can latent causal variables be identified from
observational data — and what breaks when those conditions are violated?*
