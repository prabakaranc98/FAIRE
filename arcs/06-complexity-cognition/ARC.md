## What this arc is

The intersection of AI with the science of natural intelligence — not to copy biology,
but to ask what principles of cognition, computation, and emergence are actually universal.
Dynamical systems and attractors, neural computation and coding, emergent behavior in
complex systems, and what neuroscience and cognitive science can tell us about the
design space of intelligent systems.
The core question: *what principles of natural intelligence — memory, adaptation, hierarchy,
prediction — are architecture-agnostic, and can they be instantiated in artificial systems?*
