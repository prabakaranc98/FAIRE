## What this arc is

How do generative models learn the structure of data, and can that structure be made to
represent the causal geometry of a world?
This arc spans diffusion models and flow matching as generative mechanisms, through to
energy-based models and JEPA-style architectures as world models — systems that don't just
generate, but predict and reason.
The core question: *what does a model actually learn about the world's structure when trained
to generate it, and when does that structure generalize?*
