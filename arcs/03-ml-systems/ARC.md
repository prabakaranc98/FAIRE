## What this arc is

The infrastructure layer of frontier AI — not theory, but the engineering that makes scale
possible and makes inference fast.
Distributed training (data parallelism, FSDP, pipeline parallelism), memory-efficient attention,
quantization, KV-cache optimization, speculative decoding, custom CUDA kernels.
The core question: *where are the real bottlenecks in training and inference, and can they be moved
with principled engineering rather than just hardware?*
