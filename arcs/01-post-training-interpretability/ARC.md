## What this arc is

What happens to a model after pretraining — and what can we see inside it?
This arc covers the full stack from fine-tuning and RLHF to mechanistic analysis of what those
procedures actually do to internal representations, circuits, and capabilities.
The core question: *can we trace model behavior back to identifiable internal structure,
and can we steer that structure intentionally?*
