## What this arc is

The mathematical and architectural foundations that underlie modern deep learning —
not the applications, but the mechanisms.
Loss landscapes and optimization dynamics, the geometry of learned representations,
continual learning and plasticity, self-supervised objectives, and why certain architectures
work where others don't.
The core question: *what do the mathematical structures of deep learning — loss surfaces,
gradient flow, generalization bounds — actually explain about what networks learn and forget?*
