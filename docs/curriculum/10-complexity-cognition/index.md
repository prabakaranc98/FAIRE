# Track 10 · Complexity & Cognition

> The science of computation and mind: computational complexity, emergent behavior, cognitive architectures, and the intersection of AI with theories of intelligence.

This track sits at the boundary of computer science and cognitive science — asking questions about the fundamental limits of computation, what it means for systems to be intelligent, and whether insights from human cognition can inform AI design.

## Connections to frontier research

- **Mechanistic interpretability** — using circuit analysis to understand neural network computation
- **Emergent capabilities** — why do abilities appear suddenly at scale?
- **Artificial and Natural Intelligence (ARNI)** — research programs studying intelligence across substrates
- **Consciousness and AI** — integrated information theory, global workspace, hard problem implications
