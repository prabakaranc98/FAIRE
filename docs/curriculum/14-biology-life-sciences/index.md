# Track 14 · Biology, Omics & Life Sciences

> AI for biology: protein structure prediction, genomics, single-cell omics, drug discovery, and the emerging foundation models for biological sequences.

Biology is producing some of the most striking results in AI. The combination of large biological datasets and powerful sequence models has transformed structural biology, genomics, and drug discovery. This track covers the methods and the science together.

---

### Protein & Molecular Biology

- [Protein Language Models](protein-lm.md) — ESM-2, ProtTrans, sequence representations, evolutionary signals

### Drug Discovery
- [Molecular Generation](molecular-generation.md) — de novo drug design, SMILES/graph-based generation, diffusion for molecules

- [ADMET Prediction](admet.md) — absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity prediction

### Systems Biology
- [Gene Regulatory Networks](gene-networks.md) — network inference, perturbation modeling, causal cell biology

## Connections to frontier research

- **Foundation models for biology** — large pretrained models across protein sequences, cell states, and molecules
- **AI-accelerated drug discovery** — moving from years to months for early-stage discovery
- **Causal perturbation biology** — using causal inference to understand genetic interventions
- **Multi-omics integration** — learning across genomics, proteomics, metabolomics simultaneously
