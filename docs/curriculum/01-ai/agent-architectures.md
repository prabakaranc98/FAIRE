## Why it matters at the frontier
Modern LLM agents (tool-using, multi-step reasoning, RAG systems) are direct descendants of classical agent architectures. Understanding the design space clarifies what "agentic" actually means.

## Core concepts
- **PEAS** — Performance measure, Environment, Actuators, Sensors
- **Reflex agent** — acts based on current percept only
- **Model-based agent** — maintains internal state about the world
- **Goal-based agent** — acts to achieve explicit goals
- **Utility-based agent** — acts to maximize expected utility
- **Rationality** — choosing actions that maximize expected performance
