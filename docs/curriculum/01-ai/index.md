# Track 01 · Artificial Intelligence

> Foundations of AI: search, planning, logic, knowledge representation, and the classical roots of intelligent systems.

This track covers the structural foundations that all of modern AI builds on — from search algorithms to symbolic reasoning to the emergence of learned agents. Understanding where the field came from clarifies why it is where it is.

## Connections to frontier research

Classical AI ideas are undergoing revival in the context of modern deep learning:

- **Neurosymbolic AI** — integrating neural representations with symbolic reasoning
- **LLM agents** — tool use, planning, and memory in large language models
- **World models** — learned state representations as a substrate for planning
- **Formal verification** — proving properties of learned systems using logical frameworks
